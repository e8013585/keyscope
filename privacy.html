Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

KeyScope is made by Unpossible Apps. In short: the app collects nothing, sends nothing about your PC, and goes online only when you work with a Technician License.

The KeyScope app

  • Your PC's information stays on your PC. KeyScope reads the product key and Windows details locally, entirely offline, and shows them to you. It doesn't store them, and it has no analytics, telemetry, ads or accounts.
  • Settings. KeyScope saves only your preferences (theme, language, and whether the key is shown) in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KeyScope, or in a Data folder beside the program for portable copies.
  • Reports. A report is created only when you choose Save report, and is saved only where you choose. The full product key goes into it only if you answer Yes when asked; otherwise it holds the last five characters.
  • Clipboard. When you copy the key, KeyScope asks Windows to keep it out of clipboard history and cloud clipboard sync.
  • Technician License. When you activate, check or deactivate a license, KeyScope contacts Polar's license server and sends only the license key and a random label for the installation. The license is then stored on your PC, encrypted for your Windows account (or as a file beside a portable copy).

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking of our own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and the downloads on GitHub Releases; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect its service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Purchases

Technician Licenses are sold by Polar, our Merchant of Record. Polar collects your payment details, which we never see, and processes your order under the Polar privacy policy. Polar shares with us the details needed to provide your license and support: your name, email address, country and order details. We use them only for that, and to meet tax and accounting obligations. We don't sell or share them with anyone else.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you.
